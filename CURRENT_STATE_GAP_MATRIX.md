# Casework Current-State Audit and Gap Matrix

Date: 2026-09-17

## Purpose

This is Phase 1 of `PRODUCT_AUDIT_PLANNING_PLAN.md`. It audits the shipped
product, source, hidden case definitions, scoring, feedback, progress, and
persistence before a new implementation plan is written.

This document does not authorize product implementation.

## Executive Finding

The MVP is technically sound and its six cases are internally coherent. The
case engine, answer secrecy, deterministic scoring foundation, responsive UI,
accessibility work, recovery behavior, and persistence are all worth keeping.

The main product gap is real: most important reasoning moves are selected from
authored options rather than generated by the learner. The full-case engine is
flexible enough to support several legal routes, but the authored efficient
paths are usually the same required analysis in a different order. Progress
reports scores and raw feedback codes, not recurring reasoning failures.

One additional functional gap was found in the source: exhibits can be revealed
inside full cases, and the scoring engine supports `exhibit_insight_submitted`,
but the case workspace never creates that event. A normal learner journey
therefore cannot earn the full-case exhibit score.

## Learn Audit

The ten lessons are concise, relevant, and aligned with the product's intended
positioning. They should not be replaced with longer educational copy.

The current delay from instruction to action is one navigation step, but the
linked page opens a ten-question skill sequence rather than an embedded rep for
the exact lesson. The learner can therefore read a pattern such as mix shift or
hidden denominator and receive a different first exercise. The next plan should
preserve lesson brevity and bind every lesson to one immediate, exact practice
prompt before offering the broader drill library.

### Multiple defensible paths

The engine does not force one exact investigation sequence. It accepts any
available node whose prerequisites are satisfied, separately records low-value
and repeated investigations, and scores discovered evidence rather than exact
path equality.

However, each case has one authored root cause and the two efficient paths are
usually permutations of the same required nodes. This is technical path
flexibility, not yet multiple materially different hypotheses or defensible
solutions. Preserve the engine behavior, but adapt pilot cases so distinct
reasoning routes can converge on a supported conclusion.

## Full-Case Behavior Audit

### Keep

- Server-side case definitions and learner-safe projections hide answers,
  critical flags, future facts, and calculation answers before completion.
- Investigation questions are structured, deterministic, and return authored
  interviewer responses, including useful information for low-value choices.
- Event history supports chronological scoring and recovery after refresh.
- Calculations, recommendation evidence, and stage transitions are validated by
  the authoritative server replay.
- Completed replay can safely reveal missed critical branches and an example
  efficient route.

### Adapt or replace

- Clarification responses exist in content but are not returned to or displayed
  for learners. Selecting any one high-value clarification earns full credit;
  selecting every option, including the decoy, is not penalized.
- The framework builder supports hierarchy, but the submitted case event stores
  only flattened concept IDs. Full-case scoring reconstructs every concept as a
  top-level branch, so hierarchy, order, and branch logic are lost.
- `hypothesis_selected` exists in the event schema but the engine rejects it and
  no UI uses it. Hypothesis formation and updating are absent.
- Full-case exhibit interpretation is absent from the UI even though its event
  and scorer exist. This makes the exhibit dimension impossible to earn through
  normal use.
- Synthesis can advance after one revealed fact even when the case's scoring
  rubric requires two or three pieces of evidence. A weak attempt can finish,
  but the UI does not make the weakness explicit until review.
- Feedback has only four case-level codes. `missed_segmentation` uses an
  AlpineFit-specific message about affected locations for every case, making it
  inaccurate for cases such as pricing, growth, or contract repricing.
- Review always presents the first efficient path, even though every case has
  two authored paths.

## Progress Audit

### Current behavior

- Stores the last ten scores per skill and weights the newest five more heavily.
- Shows attempt count, readiness band, score trend, and up to three common raw
  feedback codes for five skills.
- Recommends a diagnostic mix until three skills have at least three attempts,
  then recommends the weakest practiced skill.
- Always pairs the recommendation with the same AlpineFit case.

### Gaps

- Progress covers only structure, prioritization, quantitative reasoning,
  exhibit interpretation, and synthesis. Clarification and recommendation are
  scored in case review but intentionally discarded when attempts are saved.
- Feedback codes are too broad to diagnose recurring failure patterns such as
  setup error, missing implication, premature detail, overlapping branches, or
  unsupported next step.
- Recommendations select a weak skill, not a specific failure mode or the best
  next repetition for that failure.
- Timing data exists in case events but is not used for diagnosis. No spoken
  practice, confidence, self-assessment, or scaffolding level is stored.
- The current unreachable case-exhibit score can incorrectly depress the
  exhibit trend for completed cases.

## Persistence and Compatibility Audit

### Current behavior worth preserving

- Guest drill and case history, workspaces, pending saves, and scratchpads
  survive refresh in `sessionStorage`.
- Signed-in attempts and complete case event streams are stored in Supabase.
- Stable attempt IDs and idempotent writes protect retries from duplicates.
- Supabase row-level security scopes all records to the signed-in user.

### Constraints for the next plan

- Guest data is session-scoped, not durable across a closed tab/browser session.
- Scratchpad text is private browser state and is not saved to Supabase.
- The signed-in history reader loads scores but not past case events; the review
  screen still depends on the current browser session. Durable historical replay
  is not available yet.
- The database constrains drill skills and case score keys to the existing five
  skills. Adding clarification, recommendation, hypothesis, brainstorming,
  market sizing, or communication requires a forward migration and
  backward-compatible readers.
- Case attempts store `case_id` but not the case-definition version. Future
  replay or rescoring must not silently interpret old events against changed
  content.
- Existing V1 score history should remain visible after taxonomy changes, but
  it should not be presented as directly comparable if the underlying rubric or
  scaffolding level changes.

## Phase 2 Owner Decisions

Before the implementation plan is written, record binding answers to:

1. Whether the next release remains fully deterministic and no-AI.
2. How generated answers are scored, self-assessed, or compared with examples.
3. Which generated inputs remain private and which become durable evidence.
4. Whether to launch Beginner / Intermediate / Interview modes now or stage a
   smaller progression first.
5. Whether Case Opening & Clarification becomes skill six in Wave 1.
6. Whether hypothesis thinking, brainstorming, and market sizing are separate
   skills, embedded behaviors, or staged additions.
7. Which existing cases are the Wave 1 pilots.
8. How old progress is displayed after scores, diagnostics, and scaffolding
   change.

Recommended starting position: remain deterministic; use authored comparison,
structured self-assessment, and retry selection; keep private scratch work
private by default; pilot AlpineFit as beginner, PayPilot as intermediate, and
MorningJet or GoldenLoaf as a lower-scaffolding transfer case; preserve V1
history under a clearly labeled legacy scoring version.
